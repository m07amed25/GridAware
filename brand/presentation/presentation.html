The brief

GridAware is a multi-tenant B2B SaaS platform for factories and commercial buildings that connects infrastructure registries, live telemetry, threshold/anomaly detection, and traceable maintenance workflows in one calm, dark-first system.

PreciseStructuredVigilantCalmTraceable

Success criteria: unmistakable at 16 px and in the 72 px collapsed sidebar rail, works in pure monochrome and dark-first EVARAX color (#F4E952 on #080808), avoids industrial clip-art and blue SaaS clichés, and scales into a full product system.

Brand identity & logo system

Node G — Modular Brand Signature

A bold architectural G fused from a 3×3 infrastructure grid, with its upper-right cell detached as a live EVARAX telemetry node.

  • Unites Grid (structured 3×3 facility matrix) and Aware (the single monitored node caught in focus).
  • High-contrast EVARAX pairing: Pure White (#FFFFFF) + Soft Yellow (#F4E952, 15.85:1) on Near Black (#080808).
  • Engineered across optical cuts: standard master, pixel-hinted 16 px cut, and 4.3% thinned reversed white cut.
  • Unbounded Custom logotype: avant-garde wide display grotesque with sculptural ink-traps, bespoke Node-squircle i-tittle, and zero font dependency.
